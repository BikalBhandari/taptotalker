Frequently asked questions

How do I open caregiver settings?

Tap the gear button at the top of the board. If a caregiver PIN is set, enter it to continue. From settings you can change the vocabulary mode, switch card modes, and set or clear the PIN.

What are the vocabulary modes?

  • Simple — a few home cards and short paths.
  • Intermediate — the full starter vocabulary.
  • Guided — fewer cards per screen for focused choices.
  • Advanced — adds detail cards for more specific phrases.

How do I customize a card?

In caregiver settings, choose the Edit card mode. Each card on the board then shows a pencil button — tap it to change that card's label, symbol, or photo. Choose Custom to show your saved changes, or Default to go back to the built-in cards without losing your changes.

I forgot the caregiver PIN. How do I reset it?

For privacy, the PIN is stored only on your iPad and cannot be recovered by us. To reset it, delete and reinstall TapToTalker. This also removes custom cards and photos, so keep a note of your PIN somewhere safe.

There's no sound when I tap a card.

  • Turn up the iPad volume and make sure it isn't muted.
  • Check that no Bluetooth headphones or speakers are connected unexpectedly.
  • If it still doesn't speak, restart the iPad and try again.

Does TapToTalker need the internet?

No. TapToTalker works fully offline. Speech uses the voices built into your iPad.

Where is my data stored?

Everything stays on your iPad. TapToTalker has no accounts and does not collect any personal data. Read the full privacy policy.
