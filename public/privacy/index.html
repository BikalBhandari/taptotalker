Privacy Policy

TapToTalker · Effective September 28, 2026

TapToTalker is an AAC (augmentative and alternative communication) app made by LONGEST CHARIOT LLC. This policy covers the TapToTalker iPad app and the TapToTalker website at taptotalker.netlify.app.

In short: TapToTalker does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

What stays on your device

Everything you set up in TapToTalker is saved only on your own device:

  • Vocabulary and card settings
  • Custom card labels, symbols, and photos you choose
  • The optional caregiver PIN

This information is never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app (or clearing your browser's site data for the website) removes it.

Photos

If you add a photo to a card, you pick it with the standard iOS photo picker. TapToTalker only receives the photo you select, and a copy is kept on your device for that card. The app does not access the rest of your photo library.

Speech

Cards are spoken using the text-to-speech voices built into your device. TapToTalker does not record audio and does not use the microphone.

No accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking

TapToTalker has no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party SDKs. The iPad app works fully offline and makes no network requests.

Website hosting

The website is hosted by Netlify. Like most web hosts, Netlify may briefly process standard technical information (such as IP address and browser type) to deliver and secure the site. We do not use this information to identify or track visitors. See Netlify's privacy policy for details.

Children's privacy

TapToTalker may be used by children. Because the app does not collect personal information from anyone, it does not collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
